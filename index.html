Frequently Asked Questions

Who is INFORM for?

INFORM is for coaches, teachers and people who want to study their own movement. You can use it during a lesson, when preparing feedback or when reviewing your own training.

What does INFORM cost?

The current app is free to download and use. As INFORM develops, some features or services may have separate pricing. We’ll make that pricing clear before you choose to use them.

Do you store my videos?

Your local videos, drawings and notes are stored on your device. You choose what to share.

Do I need an account?

You can currently use INFORM without an account or signing in. Your local videos stay on your device.

We’re planning to introduce accounts and sign-in as INFORM develops. We’ll explain any changes to how we collect and use your information before they take effect.

What devices does INFORM work on?

INFORM works on Android phones and iPhones. The current requirements are Android 7.0 or newer and iOS 15 or newer.
