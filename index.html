How it works

Make your feedback
easier to follow

1 Record, replay and loop

Focus on the
moment that matters

Film in INFORM or open a video you already have. Slow playback and loop a key moment to examine technique together.

2 Annotate, take notes and share

Give feedback

Draw lines and measure angles directly on the video. Keep notes and coaching cues with the clip, then record your explanation and export it as a video clients can revisit between sessions.

3 Compare videos

Compare a demonstration with a client’s movement, or review clips from different sessions to identify changes in technique.

Frequently asked questions

Who is INFORM for?

INFORM is built for coaches and practitioners who study movement to refine technique—in dance, movement training and sport. Training platforms can enquire about customised integration.

Do you store my videos?

Your videos, drawings and notes are currently stored on your device, not in INFORM cloud storage. We plan to add cloud storage and will explain how your information is handled before it launches.

Do I need an account?

Not currently. We plan to introduce accounts and sign-in.

Do clients need INFORM to watch my feedback?

No. Export your feedback as a video and send it through your phone’s sharing options. Cloud sharing is planned but is not available yet.

Can we integrate INFORM into our platform?

We work with platform teams to explore custom integration. Contact us to discuss your requirements, technical feasibility and commercial terms.

What devices does INFORM work on?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer, and iPhones running iOS 15 or newer.
