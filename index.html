What it does

Get the video in. Find the moment.

Film directly in InForm, open a video already on your phone, or paste a YouTube link. On Android, you can also share straight from the YouTube app. Then take the movement apart, one frame at a time.

Slow it down until you can see it

Step one frame at a time with the arrows, or hold them to walk through the moment. Hold a speed anywhere from 0.25× to 2×. Set two markers and loop the same two seconds twenty times while it sinks in.

Drag away from the scrub bar to slow it down and land on the exact frame. Pinch to zoom into the part that matters, and mirror the view to see a move the way you would face it.

Track your movement

Advanced motion tracking of points, lines, angles and body parts. Tap a hand, a barbell plate or a whole skeleton and InForm follows it across the clip, drawing the path behind it and reading live angles at the joints.

A bar path that drifts forward, a knee that caves, an arm that drops before the punch lands: see your movement to perfect your movement.

Compare, side by side

Put two videos next to each other and play them together. A YouTube clip beside your own attempt, to learn a sequence. Today beside six weeks ago, to see whether the training is working. Sync them, loop them and step them both forward a frame at a time. A drift corrector keeps them lined up when the two takes aren’t the same length.

Students who swear nothing has changed go quiet when today is sitting next to March.

Mark it up. Measure it. Say it out loud.

Pen, line, arrow, ellipse, text and a real angle tool that snaps to square and stays accurate whatever shape the video is.

Type a note, or record your voice against an exact moment. The recording sits inside the sentence you wrote. Tap it to hear it and jump the video there.

Why I built it

A tool for the way I train and teach

As a dancer, weight lifter, rock climber and dance teacher, I was always looking for ways to study movement. I wanted a simple but powerful video analysis tool that worked the way I actually trained and taught, and I could never find the right one. So I built InForm.

From coaches and movers

What people say

Your videos

Your videos stay yours

InForm has no server. There is no account, nothing is uploaded and no analytics are collected. Everything runs on your phone, the motion tracking included. That matters if you teach, and it matters more if you teach children.

  • No account, ever. Open the app and it works.
  • Study your own videos offline. YouTube playback needs a connection.
  • You choose what goes out. Sharing uses your phone’s own share sheet, when you ask it to.

Being straight with you

What it doesn’t do

Worth knowing before you install it rather than after.

  • No cloud library or team accounts. It is a tool on your phone, not a platform.
  • Built for phones. Android and iPhone. There is no tablet layout yet, and nothing runs in a browser.
  • It will not tell you what is wrong. It shows the movement clearly. The judgement stays yours, or your teacher’s.

For individuals

Donation-based

I believe quality resources should be available to everyone who loves motion, whatever their budget. For individual users InForm is donation-ware: every feature, as much as you want, with no subscription, no ads and nothing hidden behind a paywall.

If InForm is of value to you, please donate through the Support screen in the app. A rating, a positive review and a word to friends and teammates help just as much.
