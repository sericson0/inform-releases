What devices does InForm work on?

InForm is built for Android phones and iPhones. The current requirements are Android 7.0 or newer and iOS 15 or newer. There is no dedicated tablet layout or browser version.

Can I use videos I already have?

Yes. Open a video from your phone, record directly in the app, or paste a YouTube link. On Android you can also share a link straight from the YouTube app.

Does InForm tell me how to fix my technique?

InForm helps you see and measure movement. You or your coach interpret what you see and decide what to work on.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

You can study local videos offline, including motion tracking. YouTube playback and optional online actions need a connection.

How do I send feedback or get help?
