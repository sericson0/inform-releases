INFORM — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

INFORM is a motion-analysis app for dance and sports. It films, slows down, tracks, annotates, measures and compares video.

INFORM has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data from your use of the app. Your videos, photos, drawings and notes are stored on your device and are not uploaded anywhere by INFORM. The one thing you can choose to give is an email address for occasional updates, described in Email updates below.

What INFORM stores on your device

All of the following is kept in the app's own storage on your phone, and none of it is transmitted by INFORM:

  • Videos you record in the app, and copies of videos or photos you open in it.
  • Frame captures and side-by-side comparison images you save.
  • Drawings, measurements, tracked paths and visibility timings, stored in a file beside each video.
  • Typed notes, and voice notes recorded through the microphone.
  • Projects, filming sessions and pose tags — plain dated folders on the device.
  • Settings, such as volume, layout, the open project, and any auto-delete age you set.

When you save a frame capture, INFORM may also place a copy in your device's photo gallery so it appears alongside your other pictures. That copy is yours and stays on the device.

When information leaves your device

INFORM makes network requests only in the situations below. Each one is started by something you do.

WhenWhat happens
You use dictation to speak a note INFORM hands the microphone to your device's built-in speech recognition service — on most Android phones this is provided by Google, and on an iPhone by Apple. Your spoken audio is sent to that service to be turned into text, and is handled under that provider's privacy policy, not INFORM's. The resulting text is written into your note on the device. INFORM does not receive or keep a copy of the audio. Dictation runs only while you hold the microphone button; if you never use it, no audio is ever sent.
You open a YouTube video INFORM uses YouTube API Services: when you open a YouTube link, the app requests the video's thumbnail and basic details from YouTube and plays the video using YouTube's own embedded player inside the app. YouTube and Google may collect information about that playback, including through cookies, in the same way they would if you watched the video on YouTube's website. This is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can see and manage the data Google holds at Google My Account.
You use body tracking
(optional, and only if you agree to the download)		 Body tracking needs a pose-detection model that is too large to ship inside the app. The first time you use the feature INFORM asks; only if you agree does it fetch that file — about 9 MB — from Google's model store at storage.googleapis.com. Nothing of yours is sent: no video, no frames, no account, nothing about you — only the request for the file, which necessarily includes your IP address, as any web request does. The model then runs on your device, and tracking needs no signal afterwards. Declining leaves the rest of the app exactly as it was. Tracking a point, a line or an object needs no download at all.
You sign up for email updates
(optional)		 Only the address you type, and any optional answers you give, are sent. See Email updates below for where they go and how to leave the list.
You tap a support or donation link INFORM opens your browser at that page — for example Stripe or Venmo. INFORM sends nothing with you; anything you do there is between you and that company, under their own privacy policies. INFORM has no payment processing of its own, stores no card or bank details, and is never told whether a payment happened.
You share a clip or a project INFORM hands the file to your device's share sheet and you choose where it goes. Nothing is uploaded until you pick a destination.
The app checks for a new version
(versions installed outside an app store only)		 INFORM requests a small file listing the newest available version. No information about you or your videos is sent — only the request itself, which necessarily includes your IP address, as any web request does. Copies installed from Google Play or the App Store do not make this request; the store handles updates.

Email updates (optional)

The app, and this website, can offer to send you occasional emails about new developments in INFORM. Nothing is sent unless you type an address and tap Email me updates.

  • What is collected — your email address, plus two optional answers if you give them: how you plan to use INFORM (your own training, coaching others) and your main sport or activity. Nothing else: no video, no notes, nothing about your phone.
  • Where it goes — to a small server run by the developer, which adds the address to the INFORM updates list held by Resend, the email service that delivers the messages. Resend handles the address under its own privacy policy. The server keeps no copy of its own.
  • What it is used for — occasional INFORM updates and nothing else. The address is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Leaving the list — every email carries an unsubscribe link, and one tap removes you. You can also write to InFormMotionAnalysis@gmail.com to be removed or to have the address deleted entirely.
  • What is remembered on your device — only your answer to the prompt itself: whether you signed up, said no, or asked to be reminded later. The app keeps that in its settings and this website keeps it in your browser's local storage, so the prompt does not keep reappearing. It is not a tracking cookie and it is not sent anywhere.

You can open videos and photos from your device's library whenever you like. INFORM does this through the system picker, which hands the app only the single file you chose — so INFORM never needs, and never asks for, access to your photo library as a whole.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics to measure website usage. Google Analytics is provided by Google and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The short silent previews on the demo cards are served from this site itself. Opening a demo plays it through YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed, which connects to YouTube only then and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The email updates prompt is described above. Links to Instagram and the app stores take you to those companies' sites, under their own policies.

Children

INFORM is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It has no accounts and collects no personal information from any user beyond the optional email signup above.

Your control over your data

  • Delete individual items — the app's library screen deletes a video together with its drawings and notes.
  • Free up space in bulk — the "Free Space" tool clears videos by age or by kind, and an optional auto-delete rule can do it on a schedule. It is off unless you turn it on.
  • Delete everything — uninstalling INFORM removes all of its stored videos, projects, drawings and notes from the device. Copies you saved to your photo gallery or shared elsewhere are not affected. Because uninstalling is permanent, back up anything you want to keep first, using the project backup in the app.
  • Leave the email list — use the unsubscribe link in any update, or email the address below.

Apart from an email address you chose to give for updates, INFORM holds no data about you on any server, so there is nothing else for the developer to delete, export or correct on request.

Changes to this policy

If INFORM's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed with it.
