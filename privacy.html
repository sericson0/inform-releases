InForm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

InForm is a motion-analysis app for dance and sports. It films, slows down, tracks, annotates, measures and compares video.

Personal video analysis runs on your device. InForm also offers accounts, classes, shared spaces and cloud backups, which send the information needed for those features to our service providers. Videos, photos, drawings and notes are not uploaded just because you open or analyze them. They leave the device when you choose a cloud backup, upload or sharing action, or use a service described below. Optional mobile usage analytics helps us improve InForm and can be switched off. InForm does not show advertising or sell your personal data.

This policy covers the current app's data flows. Features and service availability can differ by platform and app version. This website's separate behavior is described under This website.

What InForm stores on your device

The following is stored locally. Copies can also be included when you explicitly back up, upload or share the corresponding space or media:

  • Videos you record in the app, and copies of videos or photos you open in it.
  • Frame captures and side-by-side comparison images you save.
  • Drawings, measurements, tracked paths and visibility timings, stored in a file beside each video.
  • Typed notes, and voice notes recorded through the microphone.
  • Personal spaces, filming sessions and pose tags — folders and files on the device.
  • Settings, including volume, layout, your selected space, any auto-delete age, and your choice about optional usage analytics.
  • Sign-in session information and locally cached class or shared-space content. Account sessions allow the app to authenticate requests without asking you to sign in every time.
  • Class watched markers, last playback positions and lesson-order preferences. These learning-progress preferences stay on this device; they are not sent to the coach as a student progress report.

When you save a frame capture, InForm may also place a copy in your device's photo gallery so it appears alongside your other pictures. Copies outside InForm may also be backed up by your device or gallery provider according to your own settings.

Accounts, classes, shared spaces and cloud backups

InForm uses Supabase for account authentication, cloud database records and private cloud file storage. We use this information to sign you in, enforce access permissions, deliver content, manage subscriptions and storage limits, and operate and protect these features. Supabase processes information under its privacy policy.

  • Account information — your verified email address, account identifier, sign-in provider, and optional profile answers about your use of InForm and sport or activity. If you sign in with Google or Apple, their sign-in service supplies the identity information you authorize, which may include a name and email address. Apple may provide a private relay address. Authentication also uses session and authorization tokens. InForm retains encrypted Apple authorization tokens where available so it can revoke the authorization during account deletion. Sign-in providers process their own data under their policies.
  • Class and membership information — class or space names, descriptions, lesson titles and notes, teaching order, owner and member account identifiers, membership dates, invitations and publication times. The coach can view class membership identifiers. InForm does not provide a public student email directory. Invitation links or codes let recipients preview and request access; share them only with intended recipients.
  • Uploaded content — the videos, images, annotations, notes, voice notes and recorded feedback you choose to upload, together with information needed to store and retrieve them, such as titles, file sizes and upload status. Local source files remain on your device. Unpublished cloud drafts are stored to support preparation and resumed uploads; they are not a published lesson.
  • Who can see content — published class lessons are available to authorized class members. A student's class submission and its private coach feedback are available to that student and the coach, subject to class access. Shared-space media and feedback are available to the authorized participants. Personal cloud backups are accessible through the account that owns them. Service providers process this data to operate InForm; cloud content is not made publicly searchable by InForm.
  • Service records — storage usage, upload reservations, notification records and download authorizations. Class download authorization records include requester, owner and asset identifiers, authorized byte counts and time; these support delivery and usage reporting and are pruned after 90 days. They record permission to download, not an exact measure of playback or transferred bytes. Network services also receive connection information such as IP addresses as part of handling and protecting requests.

Personal cloud backup is an explicit snapshot and restore feature. It does not automatically upload every local edit. Uploading to a class or shared space makes the selected content available according to that space's access rules. Do not upload someone else's personal information or footage without the permission needed to share it with those participants.

Usage analytics (optional)

InForm uses Google Analytics for Firebase on Android and iOS to understand app usage and improve the app. Sharing is enabled by default when no choice has been saved. You can turn it off or on using Account → Preferences → Help Improve InForm. The choice is saved on this device, applies independently of the signed-in account and is separate from email updates. Collection starts only after the app reads your saved choice or the default. This integration does not collect Analytics on Windows.

When enabled, the SDK collects an app-instance identifier, app sessions and screen views, device and app information, approximate location derived from a masked IP address, and purchase or subscription events where applicable. InForm does not send your videos, images, annotations, notes, class content or account details as custom Analytics events, and does not set an Analytics user ID. Android advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled; the iOS integration does not include advertising identifier (IDFA) support.

Google processes the Analytics data and the developer can view usage reports. The SDK's data categories are described in Google's Android disclosure guide and Apple disclosure guide; Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Turning sharing off stops future Analytics collection; it does not erase data already sent. Analytics retention depends on the service's retention settings. For a question or request about data already collected, contact us below.

Notifications and payments

Where available, class notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on iOS, Apple's push service. Device installation identifiers and push tokens are used to deliver notifications; InForm associates a registered token with your account. You can control notification permission in your device settings. Analytics preferences do not disable account, class or notification services. See Firebase's privacy information and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Where subscriptions are offered, InForm stores plan, subscription status, billing period, provider customer/subscription identifiers and entitlement information needed to manage access and storage. Stripe handles available website/desktop subscription checkout and billing management under its privacy policy. Mobile support contributions are processed by Google Play or the App Store; InForm receives transaction results needed to confirm the purchase. External support links may open other payment providers. InForm does not store card numbers or bank account details. Canceling a subscription and deleting an account are separate actions.

When information leaves your device

In addition to accounts, cloud features, notifications, billing and enabled Analytics described above, the app uses the following network services. Account and class information can refresh automatically while those features are in use.

WhenWhat happens
You use dictation to speak a note InForm hands the microphone to your device's built-in speech recognition service — on most Android phones this is provided by Google, and on an iPhone by Apple. Your spoken audio is sent to that service to be turned into text, and is handled under that provider's privacy policy, not InForm's. The resulting text is written into your note on the device. InForm does not receive or keep a copy of the audio. Dictation runs only while you hold the microphone button; if you never use it, no audio is ever sent.
You open a YouTube video InForm uses YouTube API Services: when you open a YouTube link, the app requests the video's thumbnail and basic details from YouTube and plays the video using YouTube's own embedded player inside the app. YouTube and Google may collect information about that playback, including through cookies, in the same way they would if you watched the video on YouTube's website. This is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can see and manage the data Google holds at Google My Account.
You download an optional tracking model
(optional, and only if you agree to the download)		 Some tracking features use downloadable model files. When the app offers a download and you agree, it requests the model from its hosting service, such as Google's model store at storage.googleapis.com. The request includes connection information such as your IP address, but does not include your video or frames. The model runs on your device; footage is not sent to a cloud tracking service. Declining an optional download leaves the other app features available.
You sign up for email updates
(optional)		 Only the address you type, and any optional answers you give, are sent. See Email updates below for where they go and how to leave the list.
You tap a support or donation link InForm opens your browser at that page — for example Stripe or Venmo. The provider handles payment information under its own privacy policy. Mobile in-app contributions and subscriptions are described under Notifications and payments.
You share a clip or space through another app InForm hands the file to your device's share sheet and you choose where it goes. That destination handles the copy under its own policies. Class uploads, shared-space uploads and cloud backups are separate InForm services described above.
The app checks for a new version
(versions installed outside an app store only)		 InForm requests a small file listing the newest available version. No information about you or your videos is sent — only the request itself, which necessarily includes your IP address, as any web request does. Copies installed from Google Play or the App Store do not make this request; the store handles updates.

Email updates (optional)

The app and this website can offer occasional emails about new developments in InForm. Joining the updates list is optional and separate from signing in, class access and usage analytics. Registration can offer an email-updates checkbox; the website offers Email me updates. The address and optional answers are sent when you choose to subscribe.

  • What is collected — your email address, plus two optional answers if you give them: how you plan to use InForm (your own training, coaching others) and your main sport or activity. This signup does not send videos or notes. The service uses verification and connection information to prevent unauthorized enrollment and abuse.
  • Where it goes — to a service operated by the developer on Cloudflare, which adds the address to the InForm updates list held by Resend, the email service that delivers the messages. Resend handles the address under its own privacy policy. The service also uses temporary verification and rate-limit records. Account profile answers and email-update preferences can be stored with your Supabase account, as described above.
  • What it is used for — occasional InForm updates. The address is not sold or used for advertising; the service providers above process it to operate the list and deliver messages.
  • Leaving the list — every email carries an unsubscribe link, and one tap removes you. You can also write to InFormMotionAnalysis@gmail.com to be removed or to have the address deleted entirely.
  • What is remembered on your device — only your answer to the prompt itself: whether you signed up, said no, or asked to be reminded later. The app keeps that in its settings and this website keeps it in your browser's local storage, so the prompt does not keep reappearing. It is not a tracking cookie and it is not sent anywhere.

Children

InForm is not directed to children under 13. Accounts, cloud sharing and email updates are not intended for children under 13. Coaches and other participants must have the permissions needed before uploading footage or information about another person. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to InForm, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

Cloud retention and deletion

Cloud content and membership records are retained while needed for the account, class or shared space and its available access. Archiving a class keeps its content; it is not deletion. Deleting a cloud item or a class/shared space revokes access and queues physical file cleanup. Failed cleanup is retried, so removal from storage may take longer than removal from the app's list.

When trial, paid or payment-grace access ends, the current service allows a 30-day read/export window before cloud access is denied and content is eligible for scheduled cleanup. The app shows applicable access dates. Download or export anything you need before those dates; renewal after deletion begins cannot restore deleted files. Service-provider logs and backups may retain records according to the provider's policies. Billing or security records may be retained where needed to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute.

Removing access cannot recall files someone has deliberately exported or shared elsewhere. Managed offline copies are reconciled when the app reconnects and refreshes access; an offline device may retain a copy until then. Local source files and copies in other apps or galleries are unaffected by cloud deletion.

Your control over your data

  • Delete individual items — the app's library screen deletes a video together with its drawings and notes.
  • Free up space in bulk — the "Free Space" tool clears videos by age or by kind, and an optional auto-delete rule can do it on a schedule. It is off unless you turn it on.
  • Remove local app data — clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-managed files on phones. On desktop, folders stored outside the installation can remain and must be deleted separately. Device backups and copies in galleries or other apps follow those services' settings. Back up anything you want to keep before deletion.
  • Manage cloud content and membership — use the relevant class, shared-space or cloud controls to remove content you have permission to delete or leave a space. Deleting an owned class or shared space affects its other participants too.
  • Delete your account — choose Manage account on the Account tab, then Delete account. End an active subscription and let pending checkout expire first if the app requires it. The deletion process removes account-associated cloud content and membership records, removes the email-update registration, revokes retained Apple authorizations where available, and removes the sign-in account. It does not delete local source files or copies already exported by other people. Uninstalling or signing out alone does not delete your server account.
  • Stop usage analytics — turn off Help Improve InForm in Account preferences on each device. This controls future optional Analytics, independently of cloud services and email updates.
  • Leave the email list — use the unsubscribe link in any update, or email the address below.

You can contact the address below to ask about access, correction, export or deletion of personal information held for your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Available rights and any permitted retention depend on applicable law and the information involved.
